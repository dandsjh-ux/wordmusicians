    How to play
    • Each round has a category, like Animals.
    • Word Musicians: words slide down 5 lanes. Tap, click or press D F J K L when a word that fits reaches the line.
    • Let trick words slide past! Hitting one costs points and breaks your combo.
    • The Conductor taps words to send them down the lanes live and tries to trip everyone up. They score when players miss or fall for a trick, but they must always send more real words than trick words.
    • The Conductor role passes to the next player each round.
